## Design Context

### Users
Swift developers browsing SDK colors, comparing palettes, and copying ready-to-use accessors.

### Brand Personality
Clear, expressive, approachable. The explorer uses #FF6600 as its accent and Atkinson Hyperlegible as its font.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light editorial workbench: warm paper, ink-colored text, precise orange accents, generous color swatches and a compact inspector. Self-host the original Atkinson Hyperlegible regular and bold fonts.

### Design Principles
- Let the actual palette colors be the visual focus.
- Keep previews, code and selected color visible together.
- Preserve source accuracy and clearly distinguish unreleased APIs.
- Use keyboard-friendly native controls, visible focus and reduced-motion support.
- Make the mobile experience complete, with controls rearranged rather than removed.
